Problem: When a household needs an electrician or plumber, it usually relies on word of mouth or an unknown phone number. There is no easy way to confirm that the person is genuine, experienced or nearby, which creates a safety and trust problem.

Solution: FixNear is a location-based platform that connects customers with verified home-service professionals through two methods:

Instant Request: the customer posts a problem, and nearby available professionals can accept or reject it in real time.
Direct Booking: the customer browses nearby professionals and books one based on rating, distance and experience.

Trust layer: Professionals register on the platform and go through Aadhaar-based identity verification. Verified profiles display a badge, rating, experience, completed jobs, distance and live availability.

Current stage: A working front-end prototype covers the customer booking flow, the partner dashboard and the admin dashboard.

Planned features: Backend and database, real Aadhaar/KYC integration through a licensed provider, real-time requests using Socket.io, online payments, notifications, and a complaint and support system.

Impact: The platform also creates income opportunities for local skilled workers, who get steady, verified, nearby work.
