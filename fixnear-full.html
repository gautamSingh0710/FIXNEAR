Process

Trusted help at your door, in four steps.

Whether you need someone right now or want to pick a specific professional yourself, both ways are built in.

01

Describe the problem

Pick a category, like Electrician, then choose the exact issue — such as "fan not working."

02

See who's nearby

Get a list of verified professionals near you, with rating, distance and availability.

03

Request or book

Need it now? Send an instant request — whoever accepts first gets the job. Or choose someone yourself.

04

Get it fixed

The professional arrives and finishes the job. Rate them afterwards, and it's saved to your history.

Trust

Verification is the core of FixNear.
